Before you settle in

Which treatment should I choose?

Choose a massage for relaxation, a facial for skin care, a body scrub for exfoliation, or hair care for a fresh finish. Discuss your preferences with the spa before confirming.

How much do treatments cost?

Please confirm the price and duration of your chosen treatment with the spa before booking. The visit planner does not quote prices or take payments.

What should I bring?

Wear something comfortable and bring any questions about your treatment. Confirm any specific preparation with the spa before your visit.

Can I book online?

You can prepare and download your appointment preferences here. Online booking is not yet connected, so a plan does not reserve an appointment. Contact the spa directly to confirm availability and prices.
